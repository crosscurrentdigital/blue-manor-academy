# Blue Manor Academy — Pitch Script (scoping stage)

Target: ~3 minutes. Unlike the Piesano's pitch in this repo, **this is
scoping-stage narration, not a live demo** — no build has started, so
don't imply anything shown is already working. Aimed at Britton
LaTulippe (founder/president) or whoever handles product decisions.

**Before you pitch:**
- Don't quote founder family size or HQ location as fact — public
  sources disagree (AUDIT.md section 8).
- Don't quote current pricing/trial length without confirming — sources
  disagree on 14 vs. 30 days (AUDIT.md section 6).
- Don't quote a review-platform star rating — none was found (AUDIT.md
  section 7).
- **Ask directly, early, whether LaSoft has already been asked about a
  native app.** This is the single most important thing to know before
  going further — see beat 6.

---

## 1. Open (10 sec)

> "Hi — Claton with Crucible Lab. I've been looking at Blue Manor
> Academy, and I want to be upfront: this isn't a 'your website's out of
> date' pitch. Your platform's solid. I want to talk about the one thing
> it doesn't have anywhere."

## 2. What we found, fast (25 sec)

> "You've got a real curriculum, live Zoom classes and clubs, the
> Kids-Teach-Kids channels — a genuinely good product, and LaSoft's
> clearly done real work on the backend and dashboard. What nobody has
> today is an app. Right now, showing up to a Tuesday Art Club means a
> parent remembers to log into a browser at the right time, in the right
> timezone, for a family that could be anywhere in the world. That's the
> gap — not your platform, just the last mile of actually getting people
> to the class on time."

## 3. What we'd build (60 sec)

> "Not a rebuild — a companion app that sits next to what LaSoft already
> built, not on top of it. A 'Today' screen: what's due in the
> curriculum, what class or club is happening today, one tap to join —
> and it launches Zoom directly, with the time shown correctly no matter
> where the family lives. Push notification fifteen minutes before class
> starts. Offline access to the reading and audio content, since it's
> literature-based and doesn't need a live connection — one of your own
> reviewers already said using it on a computer all the time was more
> friction than a tablet. And joining class needs zero gate — no PIN, no
> mode to switch into. The only thing behind a lock is the Family view,
> for billing and progress."

## 4. The honest complication (25 sec)

> "Two things I want to say plainly instead of skip past. One: this
> touches kids' data and video, so there's real COPPA and app-store
> child-policy work involved — not a checkbox, a real legal review,
> priced as its own line item. Two: LaSoft's been your vendor for three
> years and clearly does good work — if they're already building a
> native app, or it's on their roadmap, that changes this conversation
> completely, and I'd rather know that now than after we've scoped
> everything."

## 5. The ask (30 sec)

> "If LaSoft isn't already doing this: $1,200 to go get real answers —
> what your systems can expose to a new app, what Zoom setup you're
> running, and a real compliance read on the kid-data side. That comes
> off the price if we move forward. The build itself would run around
> $11,500 — a home screen for today's classes and lessons, one-tap join
> done right, offline lesson access, install-and-forget, nothing new for
> your team to operate. Half to start, half at launch."

## 6. Close (10 sec)

> "Before any of that: has LaSoft ever floated a native app for you? If
> yes, that's the whole conversation right there. If not, I'd love ten
> more minutes to talk through what this could look like. Call or text
> me at 605-939-5913, or email cbutcher@cruciblelab.org."

## If they ask "why not just have LaSoft build this?" (reactive only)

> "Honestly, they might be the simpler choice — they already know your
> data model, and there's no integration risk for them. What I'd bring
> is speed and focus specifically on the Zoom/live-class experience,
> without touching your checkout, dashboard, or admin panel at all —
> lower risk to your production system than adding mobile-app work onto
> the same team that runs your core platform. If you'd rather keep
> everything with one vendor, that's a completely reasonable call, and
> I'd rather you make it with the real tradeoff in front of you than
> without it."

## If they ask "do we really need COPPA/compliance work, our site already handles this" (reactive only)

> "Possibly less than you'd think, but I wouldn't assume it without
> checking — a new app surface, even one just reading your existing
> data, can trigger its own review obligations from Apple and Google
> independent of what your web platform already does. Cheaper to
> confirm that upfront than to find out after a submission gets
> rejected."

## Do not do on this call

- Do not quote founder family size, HQ location, current pricing/trial
  length, or any review-platform rating as fact — none were confirmed
  directly this pass (see AUDIT.md).
- Do not imply anything is already built — this is scoping-stage only,
  unlike the live demo this repo's Piesano's pitch became.
- Do not promise the Zoom Meeting SDK / embedded-video experience as
  part of the default build — that's explicitly phase 2 (SCOPE.md
  section d, PRICING.md item 4).
- Do not skip asking about LaSoft's roadmap — it's the single fact most
  likely to change everything else in this pitch.
